# Broken Causeway
### A World War II Adventure for *Journey Through the Ages*

**Normandy, France — June 6, 1944 (D-Day)**

*Broken Causeway* is a World War II tabletop roleplaying adventure set during the Allied invasion of Normandy.

Players take the roles of American paratroopers from **Dog Company, 501st Parachute Infantry Regiment, 101st Airborne Division**. Following a chaotic nighttime parachute drop, the soldiers find themselves scattered across German-occupied Normandy, separated from their units and equipment.

With limited supplies, unreliable communications, and enemy patrols searching the countryside, the surviving paratroopers must regroup, navigate unfamiliar territory, and complete a critical mission: **destroy a strategically important bridge over the Douve River before German reinforcements can cross it.**

The adventure emphasizes realistic small-unit combat, teamwork, resourcefulness, and difficult decisions under pressure. Every encounter carries consequences, and success is far from guaranteed.

## About the Adventure

*Broken Causeway* is designed as an introductory adventure for **Journey Through the Ages**, a tabletop roleplaying game emphasizing realistic characters, dangerous combat, and meaningful consequences.

Although set during World War II, the adventure uses the same core rules that support the game's many historical and fictional settings.

The scenario draws inspiration from the experiences of American airborne troops during the Normandy invasion, combining historical circumstances with fictional characters, encounters, and events.

## Historical Accuracy and Representation

This adventure attempts to portray the organization, equipment, military conditions, and experiences of American paratroopers during the Normandy invasion as accurately as practical within a fictional tabletop roleplaying scenario.

**The race and gender of the military characters are intentionally limited to reflect the historical composition of the specific American airborne unit portrayed in June 1944.** These limitations are intended to maintain historical authenticity rather than represent restrictions of the *Journey Through the Ages* game system.

Other adventures and historical periods may feature different character demographics appropriate to their settings.

## Use of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial intelligence (AI) tools were used to assist with **historical research and image generation** during the development of this adventure.

AI-assisted historical research helped inform the portrayal of military equipment, uniforms, locations, and wartime conditions. AI-generated illustrations were created to support the adventure's visual presentation and atmosphere.

AI-generated content may contain historical inaccuracies. While the adventure strives for authenticity, it should not be considered a definitive historical reference.

## Historical Fiction Disclaimer

*Broken Causeway* is a work of historical fiction inspired by the events of D-Day. Although it incorporates real military organizations, locations, and historical circumstances, its characters, specific mission, and encounters are fictionalized for gameplay.

The adventure is intended for entertainment and historical roleplaying, not as a documentary account of the actions of Dog Company or the 501st Parachute Infantry Regiment.
