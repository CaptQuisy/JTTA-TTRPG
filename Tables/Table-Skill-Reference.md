## Skills Reference Table

The following table summarizes the **15 Basic Skills** including their default Attributes and a single-sentence description.

|Skill|Default Attribute|Description|
|---|---|---|
|**Academics**|INT|Represents formal education, historical knowledge, cultural understanding, and specialized academic learning.|
|**Athletics**|STR / DEX|Covers physical activities such as running, jumping, climbing, swimming, lifting, and other feats of athletic ability.|
|**Close Combat**|STR|Represents skill in hand-to-hand fighting, including unarmed combat, melee weapons, and grappling.|
|**Crafting**|DEX / INT|Covers creating, modifying, and repairing objects using practical craftsmanship and appropriate tools.|
|**Finesse**|DEX|Represents precise manual coordination used for delicate manipulation, sleight of hand, and other tasks requiring fine motor control.|
|**Influence**|PRS / STR|Covers persuasion, negotiation, intimidation, leadership, and other attempts to influence people's behavior.|
|**Investigation**|INT|Represents the ability to research information, examine evidence, identify clues, and draw logical conclusions.|
|**Medicine**|INT|Covers diagnosing illnesses, treating injuries, administering medical care, and performing medical procedures.|
|**Perception**|ACT|Represents awareness of the environment, including noticing hidden details, detecting threats, and recognizing unusual activity.|
|**Performance**|PRS|Covers entertaining or communicating through acting, music, storytelling, public speaking, and other expressive arts.|
|**Ranged Combat**|DEX|Represents proficiency with ranged weapons, including firearms, bows, crossbows, and thrown weapons.|
|**Stealth**|DEX|Covers moving quietly, remaining concealed, avoiding detection, and approaching others unnoticed.|
|**Survival**|ACT / INT|Represents the ability to navigate wilderness environments, track creatures, find resources, and endure harsh conditions.|
|**Technology**|INT|Covers understanding, operating, maintaining, and repairing mechanical, electrical, and technological systems.|
|**Vehicle**|DEX / INT|Represents the ability to operate, control, and navigate vehicles ranging from wagons and ships to aircraft and spacecraft.|

### Skill Rules — Quick Reference

**Starting Skills:** Every character begins with Level 1 in all 15 Basic Skills.

**Starting Skill Points:** Characters receive 3 Background Skill Points and 7 general Skill Points.

**Maximum Starting Skill Level:** 5.
