## Backgrounds <!-- {docsify-ignore} -->

A Background represents the life your character lived before becoming an adventurer. It reflects their upbringing, occupation, education, social standing, or personal experiences and helps explain the knowledge and abilities they bring into the story.

While Attributes measure natural talent and Skills represent learned abilities, a Background answers an equally important question:

**Who was your character before the adventure began?**

Whether you served as a soldier, earned a living as a merchant, survived as a drifter, or studied as a scholar, your Background provides both mechanical benefits and roleplaying opportunities.

A Background does not determine your character's future. It establishes their starting experience while leaving them free to develop new Skills, interests, and abilities during play.

### Choosing a Background

Choose one Background that best represents your character's previous life.

Each Background grants **3 Background Skill Points**, distributed among the Skills listed for that Background.

The following rules apply:

- Every Basic Skill begins at Level 1 before additional points are assigned.
- Each Background Skill Point increases an eligible Skill by one level.
- No more than 2 Background Skill Points may be assigned to a single Skill.
- Background points may only be assigned to the Skills listed for that Background.
- No Skill may exceed Level 5 during character creation.
- Background Skill Points are assigned before the 7 general starting Skill Points described in Section 2.3.

A Background with more available Skills provides greater flexibility, but it does not grant additional points.

**Example:** A character with the Soldier/Mercenary Background assigns 2 Background Skill Points to Ranged Combat and 1 to Survival. Both Skills begin at Level 1, resulting in Ranged Combat Level 3 and Survival Level 2.

### Backgrounds and Genre

Backgrounds are intentionally broad so they can be used across historical periods, modern settings, and science fiction.

Players may give their Background a genre-appropriate subtitle without changing its mechanical benefits.

Examples include:

|Background|Character Concept|Era|
|---|---|---|
|Athlete|Gladiator|Ancient Rome|
|Athlete|Prizefighter|1920s|
|Criminal|Pirate|Age of Sail|
|Criminal|Bootlegger|Prohibition|
|Law Enforcement|Town Marshal|Old West|
|Soldier/Mercenary|Legionnaire|Ancient world|
|Soldier/Mercenary|Paratrooper|World War II|
|Technician|Railroad Engineer|Industrial era|
|Pilot/Driver|Barnstormer|1920s|
|Pilot/Driver|Starship Pilot|Science fiction|
|Scholar|Archaeologist|Pulp adventure|
|Official|Corporate Executive|Modern or science fiction|

A Background's name and description may be adapted to the character's culture, circumstances, and era. These changes do not alter its available Skills.

The Game Master may approve customized Backgrounds when none of the existing options adequately represents a character's history.

### Background List

#### Athlete

Your life revolved around physical excellence. Whether you competed in ancient arenas, performed as a circus acrobat, played professional sports, or trained in zero-gravity competitions, years of disciplined practice developed your physical abilities.

Fame, fortune, scandal, or injury may have ended your competitive career, but your training remains valuable.

**Available Skills:** Athletics, Close Combat, Finesse.

#### Artisan

You earned your living creating, repairing, or improving the objects people depend upon.

Whether blacksmith, carpenter, tailor, gunsmith, shipwright, jeweler, or fabricator, years of craftsmanship taught you patience, precision, and practical problem-solving.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Crafting, Finesse.

#### Bounty Hunter

You tracked dangerous people for profit or obligation.

Whether pursuing fugitives, escaped prisoners, monsters, or war criminals, you learned to investigate, survive hostile environments, and capture your quarry. Patience and persistence are among your greatest strengths.

**Available Skills:** Investigation, Ranged Combat, Survival.

#### Courtesan

You navigated influential social circles through charm, diplomacy, companionship, and discretion.

You learned how to understand people's desires, influence powerful patrons, and uncover secrets concealed behind polite conversation. Your relationships may provide opportunities, obligations, or dangerous enemies.

**Available Skills:** Influence, Perception, Performance.

#### Criminal

You survived by operating outside the law.

Smuggling, burglary, forgery, confidence schemes, or organized crime may have provided your livelihood. These experiences taught you to recognize opportunities, avoid unwanted attention, and take calculated risks. Whether seeking redemption or another profitable opportunity, your underworld experience remains useful.

**Available Skills:** Close Combat, Finesse, Influence, Ranged Combat, Stealth.

#### Drifter

You have spent much of your life moving from place to place, rarely settling for long.

Constant travel taught you adaptability, resourcefulness, and how to survive unfamiliar environments. Whether wandering roads, crossing oceans, or traveling between distant settlements, you are accustomed to making your own way.

**Available Skills:** Crafting, Perception, Stealth, Survival.

#### Entertainer

You made your living captivating audiences.

Whether musician, actor, dancer, storyteller, comedian, or public speaker, performance taught you confidence, creativity, and how to command attention. You understand that timing and presentation can be as important as words.

**Available Skills:** Finesse, Influence, Performance.

#### Entitled/Nobility

You were raised among wealth, privilege, or inherited authority.

Education, etiquette, politics, and social obligations shaped your upbringing. Whether you still enjoy your family's influence or have lost your position, those experiences continue to affect how you approach the world.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Influence, Performance.

#### Explorer

The unknown has always called to you.

Whether charting distant lands, sailing unfamiliar seas, investigating forgotten ruins, or surveying distant planets, you thrive where established maps and familiar knowledge end. Curiosity and determination have carried you into remarkable places—and considerable danger.

**Available Skills:** Athletics, Crafting, Perception, Survival.

#### Journalist

You learned to uncover and communicate information.

Whether reporter, war correspondent, investigative journalist, or independent broadcaster, you developed the ability to ask difficult questions, gather evidence, and recognize stories others would prefer to keep hidden. Information is often your greatest advantage.

**Available Skills:** Influence, Investigation, Performance.

#### Laborer

Physical work and practical experience shaped your life.

Farms, factories, mines, docks, construction sites, and cargo yards taught you endurance, resourcefulness, and the value of cooperation. You are accustomed to solving problems with available tools and determined effort.

**Available Skills:** Athletics, Crafting, Survival, Technology, Vehicle.

#### Law Enforcement

You served as a peacekeeper, investigator, marshal, sheriff, detective, or military police officer.

Your duties required discipline, observation, judgment, and a willingness to confront dangerous individuals. Whether you still serve or have left the profession, your training and instincts remain.

**Available Skills:** Close Combat, Investigation, Ranged Combat, Perception, Vehicle.

#### Merchant

Trade and commerce were central to your life.

Whether operating a roadside caravan, managing international commerce, or negotiating interstellar shipping contracts, you learned the importance of information, relationships, and reliable transportation. You understand how goods, services, and influence move through society.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Crafting, Influence, Investigation, Vehicle.

#### Official

You worked within a government, military administration, corporation, or other large institution.

Bureaucracy, procedure, administration, and organizational responsibilities were part of your daily life. You understand how institutions function and how to navigate their rules and hierarchies.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Influence, Investigation.

#### Physician

Your education and practical experience taught you to diagnose illness, treat injuries, and preserve life.

Whether working in a village clinic, battlefield hospital, or advanced medical facility, you learned to make difficult decisions under pressure. Your knowledge is valuable wherever people face danger.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Medicine, Perception.

#### Pilot/Driver

You are most comfortable controlling a vehicle.

Whether operating horse-drawn wagons, racing automobiles, piloting aircraft, commanding submarines, or navigating spacecraft, you understand the challenges of transportation and maneuvering. The vehicles available to you depend on your campaign's era and technology.

**Available Skills:** Perception, Technology, Vehicle.

#### Politician

You built your influence through persuasion, negotiation, leadership, or public service.

Whether elected, appointed, or self-proclaimed, you understand how to build alliances, sway opinions, and navigate competing interests. Your experience has taught you that words and relationships can accomplish what force cannot.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Influence, Performance.

#### Sailor

The sea has been both your livelihood and your home.

Whether serving aboard merchant vessels, naval warships, pirate ships, fishing boats, or submarines, you understand life aboard a vessel and the teamwork required to survive. In science fiction settings, this Background may also represent someone accustomed to the routines and dangers of life aboard large spacecraft.

**Available Skills:** Athletics, Survival, Vehicle.

#### Scholar

Knowledge has always been your passion.

You dedicated yourself to education, research, historical study, or the pursuit of understanding. Whether historian, archaeologist, linguist, philosopher, or academic researcher, you rely on accumulated knowledge and careful interpretation.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Investigation, Technology.

#### Scientist

Your work focused on understanding the natural world through observation, experimentation, and evidence.

Whether chemist, physicist, biologist, or astrophysicist, you learned to investigate complex questions and test possible explanations. You approach problems through analysis and systematic inquiry.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Investigation, Technology.

#### Smuggler

You transported goods, information, or people that others wanted hidden.

Whether evading customs officials, slipping through blockades, or navigating criminal networks, you developed an understanding of secrecy, logistics, and discretion. Where others see barriers, you often recognize opportunities.

**Available Skills:** Influence, Stealth, Vehicle.

#### Socialite

Your influence comes from personal relationships and social connections rather than formal authority.

Exclusive gatherings, influential patrons, fashionable circles, and complicated social expectations are familiar territory. You know how to gather information while appearing effortlessly charming.

**Available Skills:** Influence, Perception, Performance.

#### Soldier/Mercenary

Military service or armed conflict shaped your life.

Whether serving a nation, corporation, private army, or mercenary company, you were trained to fight, survive, and cooperate under dangerous conditions. Discipline, experience, and loyalty may remain central to your identity.

**Available Skills:** Athletics, Close Combat, Ranged Combat, Survival.

#### Spiritualist

Faith, philosophy, or personal enlightenment guided your life.

Whether priest, monk, mystic, spiritual counselor, or wandering sage, you spent years contemplating deeper questions and helping others confront difficult circumstances. Your convictions may provide strength, guidance, and a sense of purpose.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Influence, Medicine, Performance.

#### Teacher

Your profession or calling was passing knowledge to others.

Whether tutor, professor, military instructor, guild master, or academy lecturer, you learned how to explain difficult concepts, recognize potential, and encourage improvement. Patience and communication are among your greatest strengths.

**Available Skills:** Academics, Influence, Performance.

#### Technician

You solve problems through practical knowledge and technical skill.

Whether mechanic, engineer, electrician, programmer, or systems specialist, you understand how machines and technological systems operate. When equipment fails, others often turn to you for solutions.

**Available Skills:** Investigation, Technology, Vehicle.

### Creating Custom Backgrounds

The existing Backgrounds cover a wide range of occupations and personal histories, but some character concepts may require a different combination of Skills.

With Game Master approval, a player may create a custom Background using the following guidelines:

1. **Define the Background.** Describe the character's previous occupation, upbringing, training, or life circumstances.
2. **Select three to five appropriate Skills.** Choose Skills that reasonably reflect the experience provided by that Background.  
3. **Assign 3 Background Skill Points.** Use the normal Background allocation rules, including the maximum of 2 points in any one Skill.
4. **Keep the Background era-appropriate.** Skills and training must make sense for the campaign's historical period and technology.
5. **Avoid additional mechanical benefits.** A custom Background does not grant free Edges, equipment, Attribute increases, or other advantages unless the Game Master establishes a separate campaign rule.

**Example: Firefighter**

A firefighter has experience responding to emergencies, navigating hazardous environments, operating equipment, and helping injured people.

**Available Skills:** Athletics, Medicine, Perception, Survival, Technology.

The character distributes 3 Background Skill Points among these Skills using the normal rules.

Custom Backgrounds should provide new character concepts without being more powerful than existing options.

## Backgrounds and Character Development

A Background represents the character's previous experiences, not a permanent restriction on their development.

A former Soldier may become a Physician. A Criminal may become a respected Investigator. A Scholar may learn Close Combat, while a Merchant may develop into an Explorer.

As characters gain Experience Levels, they may improve Skills and acquire Edges beyond those associated with their original Background.

The Game Master may incorporate a character's Background into the adventure through former associates, professional contacts, personal obligations, rivals, and unresolved events. These connections provide roleplaying opportunities and help establish how the character fits into the world.

**Your Background explains where your story began. Your decisions determine where it goes next.**
