# Conditions

Conditions represent temporary or ongoing effects that alter a character's abilities, movement, perception, or behavior.

They may result from injuries, environmental hazards, weapons, diseases, equipment, supernatural powers, or other special effects.

Conditions can impose penalties, restrict actions, or create additional dangers. Some Conditions are beneficial, while others represent significant impairments.

A character may suffer multiple Conditions simultaneously.

## Applying Conditions

A Condition may be imposed by:

- A successful Attack.
- A failed Defense Roll.
- A failed Saving Throw.
- A failed Skill Check.
- An environmental hazard.
- A weapon or piece of equipment.
- An Edge, power, or other special ability.

The effect imposing the Condition determines when it begins, how long it lasts, and how it may be removed.

Unless otherwise specified, a Condition remains in effect until removed through rest, medical treatment, a successful Skill Check or Saving Throw, the expiration of its duration, or another appropriate effect.

### Multiple Conditions

A character may suffer several different Conditions at the same time.

Unless stated otherwise, their effects are cumulative.

For example, a character who is both Dazed and Fatigued suffers –2 dice on affected Skill Checks because each Condition imposes a separate –1 die penalty.

**Repeated applications of the same Condition do not normally increase its penalty.**

Instead, the Game Master may extend its duration or apply a separate consequence when justified.

For example, a character who is already Bleeding does not automatically suffer two Minor Wounds per Turn simply because another attack also causes Bleeding.

A specific weapon, ability, or hazard may override this rule.

## Conditions and Dice Pools

Conditions modify the normal dice pool system.

Apply all relevant modifiers before rolling. 

For attacks made by NPC opponents, translate an attacker's bonus into an equivalent penalty to the player character's Defense Roll when appropriate.

Do not apply both modifiers for the same advantage.

## Conditions List

### Battered

The character has reached the limit of their ability to absorb minor injuries.

**Effect:**

- The character has filled all available Minor Wound slots.
- Any additional Minor Wound escalates to a Serious Wound, or higher if the Serious slots are also full.

Battered does not impose an additional dice penalty.

It serves as a reminder that the character has exhausted their Minor Wound capacity.

**Removal:** Battered ends when at least one Minor Wound slot becomes available through healing or another effect.

### Blinded

The character cannot see.

**Effects:**

- Automatically fails Skill Checks that require sight and cannot reasonably be completed through another sense.
- Suffers –2 dice on Attack Rolls.
- Attack Rolls against the character gain +1 die.
- When an NPC attacks the character, the character instead suffers –1 die on the applicable Defense Roll.

The attack bonus and Defense penalty are equivalent alternatives. Do not apply both.

The character may still perceive opponents through sound, touch, sensors, or other appropriate means.

**Removal:** Ends when the source of blindness is removed or the character's vision is restored.

### Bleeding

The character is losing blood from an injury.

**Effects:**

- At the end of each of the character's Turns, the character suffers one Minor Wound.
- The Wound escalates normally if no Minor Wound slot remains available.

#### Stopping the Bleeding

The character or an ally may spend 1 Action Point to attempt an **Intelligence + Medicine Skill Check**.

**Difficulty:** Normal (2 successes).

On success, the Bleeding Condition ends.

On failure, the character continues Bleeding.

**Removal:** Successful treatment, an appropriate medical effect, or another method capable of stopping the blood loss.

### Burning

The character is on fire or exposed to an ongoing burning effect.

**Effects:**

- At the end of each of the character's Turns, the character suffers one Minor Wound.
- The Wound escalates normally if the Minor Wound slots are full.

#### Extinguishing the Flames

The character or an ally may spend 1 Action Point to attempt a **Dexterity + Athletics Skill Check**.

**Difficulty:** Normal (2 successes).

The attempt may be performed as an Action or as an appropriate Reaction, subject to the normal Reaction rules.

On success, the flames are extinguished and Burning ends.

The Game Master may grant bonuses when the character uses water, a blanket, a fire extinguisher, or another suitable method.

Some fires may require specialized equipment.

**Removal:** Successfully extinguishing the flames, escaping the burning effect, or another appropriate intervention.

### Controlled

Another creature, device, power, or effect controls the character's actions.

**Effects:**

- The character cannot knowingly attack or directly harm the controller.
- The controller gains +2 dice on social interactions with the character.
- Additional restrictions may be imposed by the specific controlling effect.

Controlled does not necessarily mean the character has lost all independent thought or physical control.

The degree of control depends on the ability causing the Condition.

**Removal:** Ends when specified by the controlling ability, through successful resistance when permitted, or when the source of control is removed.

### Dazed

The character is disoriented, confused, or struggling to concentrate.

**Effects:**

- –1 die on all Skill Checks.
- –1 die on Attack Rolls.
- –1 die on Defense Rolls.

#### Duration

When Dazed results from a Wound, use the following general durations:

|Wound Severity|Typical Duration|
|---|---|
|Minor|1 Round|
|Serious|Several minutes|
|Critical|Several hours|
The Game Master determines the exact duration based on the injury and circumstances.

When Dazed is caused by a power, weapon, or other effect, use the duration specified by that effect.

**Removal:** Duration expires, the underlying effect ends, or appropriate treatment removes the Condition.

### Deafened

The character cannot hear.

**Effects:**

- Automatically fails Skill Checks that depend entirely on hearing.
- Cannot benefit from verbal warnings or spoken instructions unless another communication method is available.

The character may still communicate through writing, gestures, visual signals, or appropriate technology.

**Removal:** Ends when hearing is restored or the effect causing deafness expires.

### Disabled (Limb)

A limb has suffered an injury that prevents normal use.

The effects depend on the affected limb.

#### Disabled Arm

- Cannot hold or use items with the injured arm.
- Cannot perform Skill Checks requiring the use of that arm.
- May continue performing tasks that do not require the injured arm.

#### Disabled Leg

- Movement distance is reduced by half.
- If both legs are Disabled, the character immediately becomes Prone.
- A character with both legs Disabled cannot stand until at least one leg recovers.

The Game Master may allow a character to use crutches, braces, prosthetics, or other appropriate equipment to compensate for the injury.

### Distracted

The character's attention is divided or impaired.

**Effect:**

- –1 die on all Skill Checks using Intelligence, Acuity, or Presence.

Distracted does not automatically penalize physical Skill Checks unless the circumstances justify an additional modifier.

**Removal:** Ends when the distraction is removed or the effect's duration expires.

### Dying

The character has suffered a Lethal Wound and is in immediate danger of death.

**Effects:**

- The character immediately becomes Prone.
- The character cannot stand.
- The character must resolve stabilization.

Dying is a life-threatening Condition.

The character's ability to take Actions depends on the survival procedure being used.

### Fatigued

The character is exhausted from insufficient rest, prolonged exertion, or another source of fatigue.

**Effects:**

- –1 die on all Skill Checks.
- –1 die on Attack Rolls.
- –1 die on Defense Rolls.

Fatigued may result from sleep deprivation, forced marches, strenuous activity, illness, or other circumstances.

**Removal:** Adequate rest or another effect that removes fatigue, as determined by the Game Master.

### Frightened

The character is overwhelmed by fear.

**Effects:**

- –1 die on Skill Checks while the source of fear remains visible.
- –1 die on Attack Rolls while the source of fear remains visible.
- The character cannot willingly move closer to the source of fear.

The character may still defend themselves, retreat, seek cover, or take other actions that do not violate the Condition.

**Removal:** The source of fear is no longer affecting the character, the duration expires, or the character succeeds on an appropriate resistance check when permitted.

### Grappled

The character is physically held by another creature or opponent.

**Effects:**

- Movement Speed becomes 0.
- The character cannot benefit from bonuses to movement.
- The character may attempt to escape according to the Grappling rules.

A Grappled character may still perform actions that are physically possible while held.

The Game Master determines whether a particular action is restricted by the nature of the grapple.

**Removal:** The grappler releases the character, becomes incapacitated, or the character successfully escapes.

### Invisible

The character cannot be seen through normal vision.

**Effects:**

- The character is considered Heavily Obscured.
- Other characters may still detect the invisible character through sound, tracks, sensors, special abilities, or other means.
- Attacks against an invisible opponent suffer –2 dice.
- An invisible character gains +2 dice on Attack Rolls when the target cannot perceive the attacker's position.

#### Defense Against Invisible Attackers

When an invisible NPC attacks a player character who cannot perceive the attacker, the player suffers –2 dice on the applicable Defense Roll instead of granting the NPC +2 Attack dice.

Do not apply both modifiers.

Invisibility does not automatically make a character undetectable.

**Removal:** Ends when the invisibility effect expires, is canceled, or is otherwise removed.

### Paralyzed

The character is completely unable to move or act voluntarily.

**Effects:**

- Cannot move.
- Cannot speak.
- Cannot take Actions or Reactions requiring voluntary movement.
- Automatically fails Saving Throws involving Strength or Dexterity when physical movement is required.
- Attack Rolls against the character gain +2 dice.
- Against NPC attacks, the character instead suffers –2 dice on applicable Defense Rolls.

Do not apply both the Attack bonus and equivalent Defense penalty.

**Removal:** Ends when the paralyzing effect expires or is removed.

### Poisoned

Toxins impair the character's physical or mental abilities.

**Effects:**

- –1 die on Attack Rolls.
- –1 die on Skill Checks.

A particular poison may cause additional damage, Conditions, or complications.

The poison's description determines any additional effects and their duration.

**Removal:** Ends when the poison is cured, neutralized, or its effect expires.

### Prone

The character has been knocked to the ground.

**Effects:**

- May only crawl until standing.
- –1 die on Close Combat Attack Rolls.
- –1 die on Defense Rolls against Close Combat attacks.
- +1 die on Defense Rolls against Ranged attacks.

The ranged Defense bonus represents the difficulty of striking a target lying close to the ground.

If the attacker has an appropriate elevated position or other advantage, the Game Master may reduce or eliminate that benefit.

#### Standing Up

A character may stand by spending the normal Action Point cost required for the appropriate movement action, provided they are physically capable of standing.

The character must not be prevented from standing by another Condition.

**Removal:** Ends when the character stands.

### Restrained

The character is bound, trapped, or otherwise prevented from moving freely.

**Effects:**

- Movement Speed becomes 0.
- –1 die on Defense Rolls.
- –1 die on Attack Rolls.
- –1 die on Dexterity-based Saving Throws.

Restrained differs from Grappled because the character's movement is restricted by bindings, equipment, terrain, or another effect rather than necessarily being held by an opponent.

**Removal:** Ends when the restraints are removed, broken, escaped, or otherwise overcome.

The Game Master determines the appropriate Skill Check or other method for escaping.

### Silenced

The character cannot speak or produce normal vocal sounds.

**Effects:**

- Cannot communicate verbally.
- Cannot perform actions that specifically require speech or vocalization.

The character may still communicate through gestures, writing, signs, or other methods.

Silenced does not automatically prevent the character from using technology or abilities that do not require speech.

**Removal:** Ends when the source of silence is removed or expires.

### Slowed

The character's movement is hindered.

**Effect:**

- Movement distance is reduced by half.
    

Slowed may result from injury, difficult conditions, equipment, or other impairments.

If multiple effects reduce movement by half, apply the most restrictive applicable movement rule unless a specific effect states that its penalty stacks.

**Removal:** Ends when the source of reduced movement is removed or expires.

### Stunned

The character is temporarily incapacitated and unable to respond effectively.

**Effects:**

- Immediately becomes Prone.
- Cannot move.
- May speak only with difficulty.
- Cannot take Actions or Reactions requiring physical movement.
- Automatically fails Saving Throws involving Strength or Dexterity when physical movement is required.
- –2 dice on Defense Rolls against Close Combat attacks.

Electrical attacks commonly inflict Stunned.

The Game Master may determine that an action requiring concentration or coordinated movement is impossible while Stunned.

**Removal:** Ends when the stunning effect expires or is removed.

### Suffocating

The character cannot breathe.

**Effects:**

- Cannot speak.
- At the beginning of each of the character's Turns, suffers one Minor Wound.
- Additional Minor Wounds escalate normally when the Minor Wound slots are full.

#### Holding Breath

A character may voluntarily hold their breath for a number of Rounds equal to:

**Resilience + 2**

The character begins Suffocating after this period expires if breathing has not resumed.

The Game Master may shorten the breath-holding period when the character is surprised, physically exerting themselves, or otherwise unable to prepare.

**Removal:** Ends immediately when the character can breathe again.

Wounds already suffered from suffocation remain until healed normally.

### Unconscious

The character is unaware of their surroundings and unable to act.

**Effects:**

- Immediately becomes Prone.
- Cannot move or speak.
- Drops anything being carried.
- Cannot take Actions or Reactions.
- Automatically fails Saving Throws involving Strength or Dexterity when physical movement is required.
- –3 dice on Defense Rolls against Close Combat attacks.

The Game Master determines whether a particular effect can still be resisted through a nonphysical Saving Throw.

An Unconscious character may also be Dying if the Condition results from a Lethal Wound.

**Removal:** Ends when the character regains consciousness through recovery, treatment, or another appropriate effect.

## Removing Conditions

Conditions are removed according to their source and individual rules.

Common methods include:

- Successful Saving Throws.
- Successful Skill Checks.
- Medical treatment.
- Rest and recovery.
- The expiration of an ability's duration.
- Special equipment or medications.
- Removing the source of the Condition.

Unless a specific effect states otherwise, removing one Condition does not automatically remove other Conditions affecting the character.

For example, stopping Bleeding does not heal the Wound that caused it.

Likewise, healing a Wound does not automatically remove every Condition associated with it.

## Game Master Guidance

Conditions should reinforce the story rather than simply impose numerical penalties.

A Burning character may desperately search for water or roll across the ground.

A Frightened character may hesitate before entering a haunted ruin.

A Grappled character may struggle to escape while allies attempt to help.

A character suffering Bleeding may need immediate medical attention even after the immediate danger has passed.

**Use Conditions Consistently**

- Apply the listed effects rather than inventing additional penalties for circumstances already covered by a Condition.

**Avoid Duplicate Penalties**

- When two rules describe the same disadvantage, apply the effect once.
- For example, do not apply both the Prone ranged Defense bonus and an equivalent ranged Attack penalty for the same position.

**Respect Action Restrictions**

- Some Conditions prevent characters from acting normally.
- Apply those restrictions consistently with the Action Point and Reaction rules.

**Make Dangerous Conditions Matter**

- Bleeding, Burning, Suffocating, Dying, and similar Conditions can become life-threatening.
- Players should understand the urgency of treating or escaping these effects.

**Encourage Creative Solutions**

- Allow players to overcome Conditions through appropriate actions, equipment, teamwork, or clever use of the environment.
- A Condition should create meaningful choices rather than eliminate opportunities for participation.

**Conditions provide a consistent framework for representing injuries, hazards, impairments, and other effects while preserving the game's fast, player-facing resolution system.**
