# Combat Example

Reading the combat rules explains how individual actions work, but seeing them used together makes the system easier to understand.

The following example demonstrates a complete combat Round using the rules presented in earlier chapters.

It illustrates how to:

- Build Attack and Defense dice pools.
- Count successes.
- Determine Damage and Wound severity.
- Use Push Your Luck.
- Make Rushed Attacks.
- Spend Action Points.
- Use the Assess Action.
- Make Reactions.
- Apply Conditions.
- Track Wounds and combat timing.

The characters and dice rolls are examples. Actual encounters will produce different outcomes.

## The Situation

Bill's character, Sam Marlow is a private investigator working a case involving organized crime in the 1930s.

While following a lead, Sam encounters a Street Thug in a narrow alley behind a nightclub.

Sam attempts to talk his way past the Thug, but negotiations fail.

The Thug pulls a knife. Sam reaches for the sap concealed inside his coat.

Both combatants are aware of the confrontation. Neither is Surprised.

Because the player characters normally act first, Sam begins the combat Round.

**Sam Marlow**

| Statistic     | Rating                               |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Strength      | 2                                    |
| Dexterity     | 2                                    |
| Close Combat  | 3                                    |
| Athletics     | 2                                    |
| Armor         | +1 Defense die (Leather Trench Coat) |
| Weapon        | Sap (Club), 1 Damage die             |
| Action Points | 3                                    |
Sam's relevant dice pools are:

**Close Combat Attack:** Strength 2 + Close Combat 3 = 5 dice.

**Close Combat Defense:** Strength 2 + Close Combat 3 + Armor 1 = 6 dice.

**Ranged Defense:** Dexterity 2 + Athletics 2 + Armor 1 = 5 dice.

These totals may change because of Actions, Reactions, Conditions, or circumstances.

**Street Thug**

|Statistic|Rating|
|---|---|
|Physical Defense|2|
|Close Combat Attack|3|
|Weapon|Knife|
|Wounds|Minor ×2, Serious ×1, Critical ×1, Lethal ×1|
The Street Thug uses static Attack and Defense Ratings.

## Sam's Turn

Sam begins his Turn with **3 Action Points (AP)**.

He intends to disable the Thug before the situation escalates.

### Action 1: Close Combat Attack

Sam spends 1 AP to strike the Thug with his sap.

#### Step 1: Build the Attack Dice Pool

Sam uses Strength + Close Combat.

|**Source**|**Dice**|
|---|---|
|Strength|2|
|Close Combat|3|
|**Total**|**5**|

The Thug has Physical Defense 2.

Sam must roll at least 2 successes to hit.

#### Step 2: Make the Attack Roll

Bill, Sam's player, rolls:

**1, 4, 5, 5, 6**

Each 5 or 6 counts as one success.

Sam rolls **3 successes**.

Because the Thug's Physical Defense is 2, the attack hits. Sam also gains one extra success above the required Defense Rating.

**3 Attack successes − 2 Physical Defense = 1 extra success**

#### Step 3: Build the Damage Dice Pool

Damage against an opponent uses:

**2 base dice + extra Attack successes + Weapon Damage dice**

|**Source**|**Dice**|
|---|---|
|Base Damage|2|
|Extra Attack Success|+1|
|Sap (Club)|+1|
|**Total**|**4**|

#### Step 4: Make the Damage Roll

Bill rolls:

**2, 3, 5, 6**

The result contains **2 successes** and inflicts a **Serious Wound**.

The Thug suffers one Serious Wound.

#### Step 5: Determine Injury Location

The group uses the optional Random Injury Location rules.

Bill rolls 2d6 and obtains **2**.

The injury strikes the Thug's head.

The Wound Location and Condition Table assigns **Dazed** to a Serious head injury.

The Game Master describes the result:

_Sam swings the sap in a short, brutal arc. It strikes the Thug near the temple. The man staggers sideways and catches himself against the brick wall, momentarily disoriented._

The Game Master records:

**Serious Wound — Head (Dazed)**

For the duration of combat, the Thug is Dazed. The Thug remains conscious and capable of acting.

### Action Points Remaining

Sam has spent 1 AP.

**3 − 1 = 2 AP remaining**

### Action 2: Rushed Attack

Sam decides to strike again before the Thug recovers.

He spends another AP to make a second Close Combat Attack. Because this is Sam's second attack during the same Turn, it is a **Rushed Attack**. The second attack suffers –1 die.

#### Step 1: Build the Attack Dice Pool

|**Source**|**Dice**|
|---|---|
|Strength|2|
|Close Combat|3|
|Second Attack Penalty|–1|
|Opponent Dazed|1|
|**Total**|5|

#### Step 2: Make the Attack Roll

Bill rolls:

**1, 2, 3, 4, 6**

The initial roll produces **1 success**.

That is not enough to meet the Dazed Thug's Physical Defense of 2.

He decides to **Push Your Luck**.

#### Step 3: Push Your Luck

Under the Push Your Luck rules, Bill keeps dice showing 1 and 6.

He must reroll dice showing 2, 3, or 4.

The new results are:

**2, 5, 6**

The final dice are:

**1, 2, 5, 6, 6**

The final roll produces **3 successes**.

Because the reroll gained an additional success, Bill does not suffer the Overextension penalty.

Sam's attack succeeds.

**3 Attack successes − 2 Physical Defense = 1 extra success**

#### Step 4: Determine Damage

Sam's Damage dice pool is:

|**Source**|**Dice**|
|---|---|
|Base Damage|2|
|Extra Attack Success|+1|
|Sap (Club)|+1|
|**Total**|**4**|

Bill rolls:

**1, 2, 3, 6**

The result produces **1 success**.

Sam inflicts a **Minor Wound**.

The Game Master describes Sam following up with a short strike to the Thug's ribs.

The Thug now has:

|**Wound**|**Injury**|
|---|---|
|Serious|Head injury — Dazed|
|Minor|Bruised ribs|

The Thug is badly hurt but remains standing.

Wounds do not impose additional penalties beyond their associated Conditions unless another rule specifies otherwise.

### Action Points Remaining

Sam has spent 2 AP.

### Action 3: Assess

Sam considers making a third attack.

However, a third Attack during the same Turn would suffer a –2 dice Rushed Attack penalty. Instead, Sam spends his final AP using **Assess**.

He selects **Anticipate Danger**.

Sam gains **+1 die on his next Defense Roll**, according to the Assess rules.

The Game Master describes Sam adjusting his stance and watching the Thug's knife hand.

### End of Sam's Turn

Sam has spent all 3 AP.

At the end of his Turn, his Action Points refresh to **3 AP**.

These refreshed points are available for Reactions before his next Turn.

|**AP Event**|**Available AP**|
|---|---|
|Start of Turn|3|
|First Attack|2|
|Second Attack|1|
|Assess|0|
|End-of-Turn Refresh|3|

Sam's Turn ends.

## Opponent Phase

The Game Master now resolves the opponents' actions.

### Street Thug's Turn

The Thug is injured and Dazed but has not been incapacitated.

He lunges at Sam with his knife.

The Thug's normal Close Combat Attack Rating is 3.

Sam must make a Defense Roll against Rating 3.

### Sam's Defense Roll

Sam uses Strength + Close Combat to defend against the knife attack.

His trench coat provides +1 Defense die.

He also benefits from the +1 Defense die gained through Assess.

The Thug is Dazed, so that adds an additional +1.

#### Step 1: Build the Defense Dice Pool

|**Source**|**Dice**|
|---|---|
|Strength|2|
|Close Combat|3|
|Trench Coat Armor|+1|
|Assess: Anticipate Danger|+1|
|Opponent Dazed|+1|
|**Total**|8|

#### Step 2: Make the Defense Roll

Bill rolls:

**1, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 5, 6**

The roll produces **3 successes**.

The Thug's effective Close Combat Attack Rating is 2.

Because Sam rolled at least 2 successes, he successfully defends against the attack.

**3 Defense successes ≥ 2 Attack Rating**

Sam suffers no Wound.

The Game Master narrates:

_The Thug stabs toward Sam's stomach, but Sam catches the man's wrist and twists aside. The knife passes harmlessly across the front of his coat._

The Assess bonus has now been used.

### Crime Boss Enters the Fight

As Sam pushes the Thug away, a new opponent steps around the corner of the alley.

It is the Crime Boss Sam has been investigating. The Boss is holding a .45-caliber pistol.

The Boss joins the encounter during the current Opponent Phase.

His arrival is unexpected, but Sam is aware of him before the shot is fired. Therefore, Sam is not automatically Surprised.

The Crime Boss raises the pistol and fires.

**Crime Boss**

|Statistic|Rating|
|---|---|
|Physical Defense|3|
|Ranged Attack|4|
|Weapon|.45-caliber Pistol|
|Wounds|Minor ×3, Serious ×2, Critical ×2, Lethal ×1|

The Boss has **Ranged Attack Rating 4**.

Sam must make a Defense Roll.

### Sam's Reaction: Defend

Sam is at Short range from the Crime Boss.

He is standing in the open, without meaningful cover.

Sam decides to use the **Defend Reaction**. He spends **1 AP** to gain +1 die on his Defense Roll.

Because Sam's AP refreshed at the end of his Turn, he has enough AP available.

### Action Point Balance

|Event|AP Available|
|---|---|
|End of Sam's Turn|3|
|Defend Reaction|–1|
|**Remaining**|**2**|
The Reaction bonus applies to this Defense Roll.

### Sam's Defense Roll Against the Pistol

Sam uses Dexterity + Athletics to avoid the gunshot.

The trench coat's armor bonus applies to most close combat assaults, but does nothing to protect against a 45 round. 

#### Step 1: Build the Defense Dice Pool

| Source          | Dice  |
| --------------- | ----- |
| Dexterity       | 2     |
| Athletics       | 2     |
| Defend Reaction | +1    |
| **Total**       | **5** |

Sam must roll **4 successes** to avoid the Crime Boss's attack completely.

#### Step 2: Make the Defense Roll

Bill rolls:

**1, 2, 4, 5, 6**

The initial roll produces **2 successes**.

That is not enough to avoid the gunshot.

Bill decides to Push Your Luck.

#### Step 3: Push Your Luck

Bill keeps the 1, 5, and 6.

He rerolls the 2, and 4.

The reroll produces:

**2, 5**

The final dice are:

**1, 2, 5, 5, 6**

The final roll produces **3 successes**.

Bill gained one additional success, so he does not suffer the Overextension penalty.

However, 3 successes are still not enough to meet the Crime Boss's Attack Rating of 4.

#### Step 4: Determine the Wound

When an opponent attacks a player character, the player does not make a separate Damage Roll.

Instead, the Defense shortfall determines the Wound.

**Defense Shortfall = Attack Rating − Defense successes**

For this attack:

**4 − 3 = 1**

A shortfall of 1 causes a **Minor Wound**.

Sam is hit, but his defensive effort prevents a more serious injury.

The Game Master describes the result:

_Sam throws himself sideways as the pistol fires. The bullet grazes his upper arm, tearing through his coat and leaving a painful wound. He crashes against the alley wall but remains on his feet._

Sam records **one Minor Wound**.

## End of Round 1

All combatants have completed their Turns.

The Game Master reviews the current situation.

**Sam Marlow**

- Has suffered one Minor Wound.
- Has 2 AP remaining after spending 1 AP on the Defend Reaction.
- Is standing in the open.
- Has used his Assess bonus.
- Is facing two armed opponents.

**Street Thug**

- Has suffered one Serious Wound to the head.
- Has suffered one Minor Wound to the ribs.
- Is Dazed.
- Has reduced Physical Defense and Close Combat Attack Ratings while Dazed remains active.

**Crime Boss**

- Has suffered no Wounds.
- Is armed with a .45-caliber pistol.
- Has joined the fight during the Opponent Phase.
- Is positioned at Short range from Sam.

The first combat Round ends.

## Beginning Round 2

The next combat Round begins.

Sam normally acts during the Player Phase before the opponents. However, Sam spent 1 AP on a Defend Reaction after his previous Turn ended. His AP do not refresh at the beginning of his new Turn. He therefore begins Round 2 with **2 AP available**. 

At the end of that Turn, his AP will refresh to 3 again.

Sam must decide whether to:

- Attack the wounded Thug.
- Seek cover from the Crime Boss.
- Attempt to escape the alley.
- Use a tactical Action to improve his position.
- Negotiate or surrender.
- Try another creative solution.

The Game Master asks Bill what Sam does next.

The encounter continues.

## Game Master Guidance

A worked combat example should make the rules easier to follow.

During play, the Game Master does not need to announce every calculation or explain each rule in detail. Once players become familiar with the system, most Actions can be resolved quickly. Describe the situation, ask what the characters do, establish the appropriate roll, and narrate the result.

The most important principles are:

- Players make the dice rolls.
- Opponents use static Attack and Defense Ratings.
- Every Attack and Defense success matters.
- Damage and Wounds are resolved consistently.
- Actions and Reactions consume Action Points.
- Conditions create tactical consequences.
- The fiction should guide the rules, and the rules should support the fiction.

**Combat should remain fast, dangerous, and cinematic while giving players meaningful tactical choices.**
