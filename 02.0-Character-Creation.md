# Character Creation

Character creation is the process of bringing a new hero to life. Every character is defined by their natural abilities, personal history, learned skills, unique talents, and personal weaknesses. Together, these elements determine how the character approaches challenges, interacts with others, and survives the dangers of the world.

Whether your campaign takes place during the age of piracy, the trenches of the First World War, the streets of a modern city, or the frontier of deep space, the same character creation rules apply.

Characters in _Journey Through the Ages_ are capable individuals whose abilities develop through training, experience, and determination. They may possess exceptional talents, but they remain subject to the dangers and limitations of their world.

Character creation follows seven steps.

## Character Creation Summary

| Step                              | Description                                                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Determine Attributes           | Assign ratings to six Attributes using the point-buy or random generation method.                    |
| 2. Choose a Background            | Select a Background representing the character's previous life and assign 3 Background Skill Points. |
| 3. Select Skills                  | Begin with Level 1 in all Basic Skills and distribute 7 additional Skill Points.                     |
| 4. Choose Edges                   | Select a Level 1 Edge representing a special talent or advantage.                                    |
| 5. Choose Hindrances (Optional)   | Select up to 2 levels of Hindrances to gain additional Edge Points.                                  |
| 6. Create a Character Description | Describe the character's appearance, personality, motivations, and history.                          |
| 7. Select Starting Equipment      | Determine starting Wealth and choose equipment appropriate to the campaign.                          |
|                                   |                                                                                                      |
Each step builds upon the previous one, resulting in a complete character ready for adventure.

### Step 1: Determine Attributes

Attributes represent the character's natural physical, mental, and social capabilities. They form the foundation for Skill Checks, Combat, Saving Throws, and other actions.

Every character possesses six Attributes:

- **Strength (STR):** Physical power and muscular capability.
- **Dexterity (DEX):** Coordination, agility, balance, and reflexes.
- **Resilience (RES):** Physical endurance, toughness, and resistance to injury.
- **Intelligence (INT):** Reasoning, knowledge, memory, and problem-solving.
- **Acuity (ACT):** Awareness, perception, intuition, and mental alertness.
- **Presence (PRS):** Confidence, personality, leadership, and social influence.

Attributes are rated from **1 to 4**, with 2 representing average human capability.

Players may determine their Attributes using either the standard **13-point allocation method** or the optional random generation method described in Section 2.1.

Attributes normally remain unchanged throughout a character's career. Characters improve primarily by developing Skills and acquiring Edges.

### Step 2: Choose a Background

A Background represents the character's life before becoming an adventurer. It reflects their upbringing, profession, education, social standing, or previous experiences.

Backgrounds help establish a character's place in the world and provide a foundation for their starting Skills.

Each Background grants **3 Background Skill Points**, which may be distributed among the Skills listed for that Background.

No more than **2 Background Skill Points** may be assigned to any single Skill.

Background Skill Points are assigned before the character's general starting Skill Points. No Skill may exceed Level 5 during character creation.

Backgrounds are intentionally broad and can represent different occupations across historical periods and genres. For example, a Soldier/Mercenary Background might describe a Roman legionary, a medieval mercenary, a World War II paratrooper, or a futuristic marine.

### Step 3: Select Skills

Skills represent learned knowledge, practical experience, and specialized training.

While Attributes describe a character's natural capabilities, Skills represent what the character has learned to do.

Every character begins with **Level 1 in all Basic Skills**, representing ordinary experience and general competence.

After assigning Background Skill Points, the player receives **7 additional Skill Points** to distribute among Basic Skills.

### Step 4: Choose Edges

Edges represent special talents, advanced training, exceptional abilities, or advantages that distinguish a character from others. Two characters with identical Attributes and Skills may behave very differently because of their Edges.

Every starting character receives **1 Edge Point**, which may be used to acquire a Level 1 Edge.

Most Edges have two levels:

- **Level 1:** Provides a useful advantage or special capability.
- **Level 2:** Improves or expands the Edge's benefits.

A character must possess Level 1 before acquiring Level 2 of the same Edge.

### Step 5: Choose Hindrances (Optional)

Hindrances represent physical limitations, personal weaknesses, behavioral flaws, or other disadvantages that create challenges for the character. They help distinguish characters from one another and provide opportunities for meaningful roleplaying.

A character may select up to **2 levels of Hindrances** during character creation. Each Hindrance level grants **1 additional Edge Point**, allowing the player to acquire another Edge or improve an existing Edge.

Hindrances are optional, but create meaningful limitations without making the character incapable of participating in the adventure.

### Step 6: Create a Character Description

Once the character's mechanical abilities have been established, the player develops the person behind those numbers.

A character description may include:

- **Physical Appearance:** Age, height, build, clothing, and distinguishing features.
- **Personality:** Attitudes, habits, temperament, and behavior.
- **Motivation:** The reasons the character undertakes dangerous adventures.
- **Background Story:** Important events that shaped the character's life.
- **Relationships:** Family, friends, rivals, mentors, and other important people.
- **Ideals and Beliefs:** Personal values, loyalties, and convictions.
- **Fears and Weaknesses:** Personal struggles and vulnerabilities.
- **Personal Goals:** What the character hopes to accomplish.

Players should focus on details that help bring the character to life rather than attempting to write an extensive biography. A few meaningful details are often enough to create a memorable character.

### Step 7: Select Starting Equipment

Every character begins with clothing, personal belongings, and equipment appropriate to their Background and the campaign setting.

The Game Master determines the starting Wealth available to characters and establishes any restrictions based on the historical period, technology, occupation, or circumstances of the adventure.

Players then select appropriate equipment, including weapons, tools, protective gear, medical supplies, communication devices, and other useful items. Equipment must be suitable for the character's era and reasonably accessible under the circumstances.

Characters should begin with enough equipment to fulfill their intended roles without eliminating the importance of preparation, resources, and equipment acquisition during play.

## Before You Begin

Before finalizing a character, the player and Game Master should agree on the campaign's historical period, genre, tone, and expectations.

These decisions determine which Backgrounds, Skills, Edges, Hindrances, and equipment are appropriate.

The Game Master should also establish whether the campaign includes optional extraordinary abilities, such as Psionic powers, supernatural talents, or advanced speculative technology.

### Final Character Review

Before beginning play, confirm that:

1. All six Attributes have been assigned legally.
2. A Background has been selected and its 3 Skill Points distributed.
3. Every Basic Skill begins at Level 1, with 7 additional Skill Points assigned using the standard method, or 10 using the optional escalating-cost method.
4. Starting Edges and any additional Edge Points from Hindrances have been assigned legally.
5. No more than 2 levels of Hindrances have been selected.
6. The character's Minor Wound slots equal their Resilience rating, subject to applicable modifiers and a minimum of one.
7. The character's description, starting equipment, and Wealth have been recorded.

The character begins at **Experience Level 1**. Additional Experience Levels and Experience Points are earned during play, as described in Section 2.8.

Once these steps are complete, the character is ready to begin their adventure.
