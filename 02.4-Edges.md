## Edges <!-- {docsify-ignore} -->

Edges represent exceptional talents, specialized training, natural gifts, and unusual experiences that distinguish one character from another.

While Attributes describe natural aptitude and Skills measure training, Edges provide advantages beyond ordinary competence.

Some Edges reflect years of dedicated practice. Others represent unusual luck, physical capability, social influence, mental discipline, or valuable connections.

An Edge should make a character more effective in particular circumstances without eliminating the need for Skill Checks or meaningful decisions.

### Choosing Edges

Every character begins with **1 Edge Point** during character creation.

A character may receive up to **2 additional Edge Points** by selecting Hindrances.

Each Edge Point purchases one Edge level.

Most Edges have two levels:

- **Level 1:** An uncommon talent, useful advantage, or specialized capability.
- **Level 2:** Exceptional mastery that improves or expands the Level 1 benefit.

A character must acquire Level 1 before purchasing Level 2.

Unless specifically stated otherwise, Level 2 replaces the corresponding Level 1 numerical benefits. The bonuses are not added together.

Different Edges may provide cumulative benefits when their conditions independently apply. All modifiers remain subject to the normal dice-pool limits.

### Edge Availability

Edges are identified by when they may be acquired.

**Character Creation Only:** These Edges represent natural gifts, inherent traits, or formative experiences. They must be acquired during character creation. A character who already possesses Level 1 may later improve it to Level 2, with Game Master approval.

**Character Creation or Advancement:** These Edges represent abilities that can be learned or developed through experience. They may be acquired during character creation or through normal advancement.

### Using Edge Bonuses

Unless an Edge specifically states otherwise:

- A +1 or +2 bonus adds that number of dice to the applicable roll.
- Bonuses apply only under the circumstances described.
- Edge benefits do not automatically guarantee success.
- An Edge cannot reduce a task's Difficulty unless it specifically states that it does so.
- A benefit that grants a free action or reaction is an exception to the normal Action Point rules and must specify its timing.

### Edge Rerolls

Many Edges allow a character to reroll one or more dice.

When an Edge grants a reroll:

1. Make the original roll.
2. After seeing the results, choose eligible dice showing 2, 3, or 4.
3. Reroll up to the number of dice permitted by the Edge.
4. Keep the new results, even if they are worse.

Dice showing 1, 5, or 6 cannot be rerolled through these standard Edge benefits.

An Edge reroll does not count as a separate Push Your Luck attempt. Any new 1s rolled still count toward determining Critical Failure.

Edge reroll frequency:

**Once per Scene:** A benefit may be used once during a meaningful encounter or narrative scene. It refreshes when the GM begins a new scene.

**Once per Session:** A benefit may be used once during a game session.

**Once per Day:** A benefit refreshes after a full day has passed in the game world.

**Once per Turn:** A benefit may be used once during the character's turn.

**Once per Round:** A benefit may be used once during the current combat round, including another character's turn when permitted.

### Edge Format

Each Edge includes:

**Name:** The name of the Edge.

**Level:** Level 1 or Level 2.

**Availability:** Character Creation Only or Character Creation or Advancement.

**Description:** The talent or advantage represented.

**Effect:** The mechanical benefits provided.

### Categories of Edges

Edges are organized into six categories:

1. Combat & Reflexes
2. Stealth & Deception
3. Mental & Cognitive Abilities
4. Luck & Probability Manipulation
5. Physical Prowess, Endurance & Survival
6. Persuasion & Influence

These categories are organizational and do not restrict character selection.

#### Combat & Reflexes

These Edges improve combat awareness, accuracy, coordination, defensive capability, and specialized fighting techniques.

##### Battlefield Awareness

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Perception Checks to detect ambushes and traps. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. When you successfully detect an ambush or trap, you may immediately warn nearby allies who can hear or see you.

##### Blind Fighting

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Reduce the dice-pool penalty imposed on your attacks by darkness or the Blinded condition by 1 die, to a minimum penalty of zero.

**Level 2:** Ignore attack penalties caused solely by darkness or the Blinded condition when the target's location is otherwise known. You may use hearing and other available senses to locate nearby creatures, subject to an appropriate Perception Check.

This Edge does not permit sight-dependent activities, such as reading, and does not automatically reveal hidden opponents.

##### Brawler

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Close Combat Checks when making unarmed strikes. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

##### Command

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Once per combat round, you may provide a Help reaction to an ally without spending an Action Point, provided you can communicate useful instructions and the normal conditions for Help are satisfied.

**Level 2:** When using this free Help reaction, grant the ally +2 dice instead of the normal bonus provided by the reaction.

The free Help reaction remains subject to the normal restriction on using the same Reaction type more than once per round.

##### Deadeye Marksman

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Ranged Combat attacks made at Long range or greater. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such an attack.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

##### Enhanced Reflex Training

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on your first Defense roll during combat, provided it occurs before your first turn.

**Level 2:** Increase this bonus to +2 dice. You may also use one normally available Reaction before your first turn without spending an Action Point, once per combat.

This Edge does not grant an additional attack or alter the normal initiative order.

##### Escape Artist

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on checks to escape bindings, grapples, or confined spaces. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

##### Martial Arts

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** You may use Dexterity instead of Strength for Close Combat Checks involving unarmed fighting or suitable martial arts weapons.

**Level 2:** When using Dexterity for these Close Combat Checks, gain +1 additional die. You also gain +1 die on Ranged Combat attacks with thrown knives, axes, spears, or similar weapons appropriate to your training.

Thrown weapon attacks still use Ranged Combat.

##### Protective Instinct

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Once per combat round, when an ally within 5 feet makes a Defense roll, you may grant that ally +1 Defense die without spending an Action Point.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 Defense dice.

You must be conscious, aware of the attack, and physically capable of assisting. This ability does not move the ally or transfer the attack to you.

##### Weapon Master

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

Choose one specific weapon type when acquiring this Edge.

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on attacks using the chosen weapon type. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such an attack.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

A weapon type should be specific enough to represent meaningful specialization, such as longswords, revolvers, or combat knives.

##### Wrestler

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Close Combat Checks involving grappling, holding, pinning, or escaping a grapple. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

The character may attempt grapples against larger opponents, but the normal size and circumstance modifiers still apply.

#### Stealth & Deception

These Edges help characters conceal their identities, infiltrate secure locations, navigate unfamiliar societies, and exploit clandestine connections.

##### Expert Locksmith

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Finesse Checks to pick mechanical locks or bypass physical locking mechanisms. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. You may attempt unusually sophisticated mechanical locks without additional penalties solely for their complexity.

Electronic security systems normally require Technology rather than Finesse.

##### Master Disguise

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Performance Checks to conceal your identity or impersonate another person through disguise, mannerisms, or voice. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

Disguises still require appropriate materials and cannot automatically overcome close inspection or contradictory evidence.

##### Social Chameleon

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Influence Checks when attempting to fit into an unfamiliar social group or culture. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

This Edge does not grant knowledge of languages or customs the character has never encountered.

##### Underworld Connections

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Influence Checks when seeking illegal information, black-market goods, or cooperation from criminal contacts. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. Once per session, you may request a significant favor from a criminal contact.

The contact's willingness and ability to help depend on the circumstances. Favors may carry obligations, costs, or risks.

#### Mental & Cognitive Abilities

These Edges represent exceptional memory, concentration, courage, and intellectual aptitude.

##### Enhanced Memory

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Academics Checks to recall previously learned information. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. You can recall ordinary details of previously studied documents or witnessed events without a roll when sufficient attention was originally paid.

Perfect recall does not reveal information the character never learned.

##### Fearless

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +2 dice on Saving Throws against fear and panic. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** You automatically resist ordinary, non-supernatural fear that would otherwise require a Saving Throw. Against supernatural fear or similar extraordinary effects, gain +2 dice and may reroll 2 eligible dice once per scene.

##### Focused Mind

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Saving Throws against the Controlled, Dazed, or Distracted conditions when caused by mental interference. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. The bonus also applies to Saving Throws against Psionic intrusion and other effects that directly impair mental control.

##### Linguistic Savant

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Learn unfamiliar languages more quickly than ordinary students. Gain +1 die on appropriate Academics or Investigation Checks to interpret unfamiliar language patterns. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. After meaningful exposure, you can recognize basic grammatical structures and communicate simple concepts in unfamiliar languages, subject to GM judgment.

This Edge does not automatically decipher encrypted messages or translate languages for which the character has no meaningful information.

#### Luck & Probability Manipulation

These Edges represent remarkable fortune, intuition, and the ability to capitalize on favorable circumstances.

##### High Roller

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on appropriate checks involving games of chance, reading gambling opponents, or bluffing during wagers. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

##### Lucky

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Once per in-game day, after making a Skill Check or Combat Check, reroll up to 2 dice showing 2, 3, or 4. This does not trigger Push Your Luck overextension.

**Level 2:** Increase the allowance to 3 dice. Once per in-game day, you may use this ability even after already using Push Your Luck, provided the selected dice have not previously been rerolled.

Lucky does not automatically convert failures into successes and cannot change dice showing 1, 5, or 6.

##### Wealth

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Your financial resources exceed those of an ordinary person. Increase your Wealth by one category, subject to the Wealth rules and Game Master approval.

**Level 2:** Increase your Wealth by two categories above the rating you would otherwise possess.

The bonus represents income, assets, investments, property, or access to financial support.

Wealth does not automatically grant restricted equipment, military hardware, spacecraft, or resources unavailable in the campaign setting. Acquisitions remain subject to availability, legality, and the established Wealth rules.

#### Physical Prowess, Endurance & Survival

These Edges improve physical performance, endurance, navigation, tracking, and the ability to survive dangerous environments.

##### Ace Tracker

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Survival Checks to track creatures or interpret signs of passage. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. The bonus also applies to Perception or Investigation Checks made specifically to interpret tracks or related evidence.

##### Ambidextrous

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Reduce the normal off-hand penalty by 1 die.

**Level 2:** Ignore the normal off-hand penalty when using either hand.

This Edge does not eliminate penalties for fighting with two weapons simultaneously.

##### Athletic Build

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Athletics Checks involving lifting, carrying, or pushing heavy objects.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice. Increase ordinary jumping and climbing distances by 50% when sufficient space and suitable conditions permit.

This Edge does not increase the character's Strength Attribute.

##### Daredevil

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Athletics or Vehicle Checks involving dangerous stunts or acrobatic maneuvers. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. Once per session, ignore up to 2 dice of situational penalties on a qualifying stunt.

The GM determines whether the attempted action is physically possible.

##### Expert Climber

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Athletics Checks involving climbing. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check. When climbing a suitable surface, move at three-quarters of your normal movement rate rather than the usual rate for Difficult Terrain.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. On suitable surfaces, climb at your normal movement rate.

Hazardous surfaces, insufficient handholds, and extreme conditions may still require checks or impose additional penalties.

##### Explorer's Instinct

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Perception Checks to detect hidden paths, hazards, or unusual environmental details in wilderness areas or ruins. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. Once per session, when an ambush or environmental trap is about to occur, the GM may allow you an immediate Perception Check to recognize the danger before it is resolved.

##### Iron Constitution

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Resilience Saving Throws against poison, toxins, alcohol, disease, or physical exhaustion. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

##### Navigator

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Survival Checks involving navigation and route-finding. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. Once per session, you may make an additional attempt after failing a navigation check, provided new information or a plausible opportunity to reassess the route is available.

##### Resourceful Scavenger

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Investigation or Survival Checks when searching for useful supplies or salvage. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. You may also use Crafting or Technology to repurpose recovered materials, subject to the normal requirements for tools, materials, and time.

##### Tireless

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Saving Throws against fatigue, sleep deprivation, forced marches, and prolonged physical exertion. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

#### Persuasion & Influence

These Edges represent exceptional social confidence, negotiation skills, leadership, and access to useful relationships.

##### Animal Handler

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Influence, Survival, or Vehicle Checks involving calming, directing, training, or riding domesticated animals and trained creatures. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. You can recognize obvious signs of an animal's temperament, distress, or training through observation.

Wild or hostile animals may still resist handling.

##### Bureaucratic Connections

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Influence Checks to gather information, obtain permits, or navigate administrative procedures through official contacts. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. Once per session, you may request a significant administrative favor from an established contact.

A favor cannot automatically override laws, institutional authority, or the contact's personal interests.

##### Charismatic Negotiator

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Influence Checks involving bargaining, purchasing, selling, or negotiating agreements. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

##### Resonant Voice

**Availability:** Character Creation Only

**Level 1:** Gain +1 die on Influence or Performance Checks when addressing a crowd through speech or vocal performance. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

**Level 2:** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice.

This Edge does not eliminate language barriers or allow the character to be heard beyond normal physical limits.

##### Well-Connected

**Availability:** Character Creation or Advancement

**Level 1:** Once per session, you may identify and contact a useful acquaintance who could reasonably provide information, introductions, or a minor favor.

**Level 2:** Your network is more extensive. Once per session, you may request a substantial favor involving transportation, equipment, introductions, or privileged information.

Contacts must be plausible within the campaign setting. Their cooperation may require payment, persuasion, reciprocal assistance, or accepting an obligation.

### Creating Custom Edges

The Edges presented here are not intended to cover every exceptional talent or specialized capability a character might possess.

With Game Master approval, players may create new Edges appropriate to their characters and campaign.

A custom Edge should provide a distinctive benefit beyond ordinary Skill training while remaining narrow enough to preserve meaningful challenges.

#### Level 1 Edges

A Level 1 Edge should normally provide one or more of the following:

- +1 die on a narrowly defined category of Skill Checks.
- The ability to reroll 1 eligible die once per scene.
- A minor exception to an established rule.
- Access to useful contacts or specialized opportunities.
- A limited capability unavailable through ordinary Skill use.

**Example — Experienced Rider (Level 1):** Gain +1 die on Vehicle Checks when riding animals. Once per scene, reroll 1 eligible die on such a check.

#### Level 2 Edges

A Level 2 Edge represents exceptional expertise and should improve upon Level 1.

Typical benefits include:

- Increasing a +1 die bonus to +2 dice.
- Increasing a reroll allowance from 1 die to 2 dice.
- Expanding the situations in which the Edge applies.
- Providing a significant but limited additional capability.

**Example — Experienced Rider (Level 2):** Increase the bonus to +2 dice and the reroll allowance to 2 dice. The character may also ignore 1 die of situational penalties caused by difficult riding conditions.

#### Guidelines for Custom Edges

**Keep Benefits Specific.** Avoid Edges that grant bonuses to an entire Attribute or every use of a broad Skill.

**Preserve Meaningful Checks.** An Edge should normally improve the chance of success rather than guarantee it.

**Respect Action Economy.** Free attacks, reactions, and additional actions are especially powerful. Such benefits should be narrowly defined and limited in frequency.

**Avoid Automatic Immunity.** Immunity to entire categories of threats should be rare and clearly limited.

**Use Existing Mechanics.** Prefer bonus dice, rerolls, reduced penalties, and existing Action or Reaction types over introducing new resolution procedures.

**Consider the Setting.** A historical campaign may emphasize practical training and social connections, while science fiction may introduce specialized technological advantages.

**Require Game Master Approval.** The GM may revise a custom Edge that proves too powerful, too restrictive, or unclear during play.

The purpose of an Edge is to make a character distinctive—not to remove uncertainty or bypass the rules that make an adventure challenging.
