
# Acknowledgments & Inspiration

_Journey Through the Ages_ was inspired by many tabletop roleplaying games and the designers who created them. Their approaches to character creation, skill-based play, action, narrative flexibility, and game design helped inspire the development of this system.

Notable inspirations include **Nimble, Tales from Elsewhere, Shadowrun, the Year Zero Engine, Outgunned & Outgunned Adventures, Coriolis,** and **Stars Without Number**.

_Journey Through the Ages_ is an independent work and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the creators or publishers of these games. All referenced game titles and trademarks remain the property of their respective owners.
