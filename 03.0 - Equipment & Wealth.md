# Equipment & Wealth

The right equipment can mean the difference between success and failure, or even life and death.

A reliable weapon, protective armor, a well-stocked medical kit, or the proper climbing gear can provide advantages that skill and determination alone cannot overcome.

Equipment is an essential part of every adventure. It allows characters to confront dangerous opponents, explore unfamiliar environments, overcome obstacles, and survive situations for which they might otherwise be unprepared.

In _Journey Through the Ages_, equipment reflects the technology, resources, and circumstances of the campaign's historical period.

A pirate may depend on a cutlass, flintlock pistol, and navigational instruments. A World War II soldier might carry a rifle, field equipment, and medical supplies. A futuristic explorer may rely on advanced communications, protective suits, and sophisticated survival technology.

Although the equipment changes between eras, the game uses a consistent set of core mechanics to resolve its use.

## The Right Tool for the Job

Characters often encounter challenges that cannot be overcome through natural ability or training alone.

A skilled physician needs medical supplies to perform surgery. An experienced mechanic requires suitable tools to repair a damaged engine. An expert climber may need ropes and specialized equipment to scale a dangerous cliff.

Equipment can influence an action in several ways:

- **Enabling an Action:** Some tasks require specific tools, supplies, or equipment before they can reasonably be attempted.
- **Improving Performance:** Appropriate equipment may provide bonuses or reduce penalties when the applicable rules permit.
- **Providing Protection:** Armor, shields, and other protective equipment help characters withstand attacks and environmental hazards.
- **Expanding Capabilities:** Vehicles, communication devices, and specialized tools allow characters to perform activities that would otherwise be impossible.
- **Creating Limitations:** Equipment may be heavy, cumbersome, fragile, difficult to conceal, or dependent on ammunition, fuel, and other supplies.

Possessing equipment does not automatically guarantee success.

Characters must still possess the necessary training, use their equipment appropriately, and overcome the challenges presented by their circumstances.

The Game Master determines when equipment is required and applies the relevant rules for its use.

## Equipment Across the Ages

_Journey Through the Ages_ supports adventures in historical, modern, and futuristic settings.

Equipment must be appropriate to the campaign's era and technological development.

A medieval adventurer cannot ordinarily purchase a modern firearm, while a contemporary character would not have access to advanced spacecraft technology unless the setting specifically permits it.

Even when an item exists, it may be rare, restricted, prohibitively expensive, or unavailable in a particular location.

The Game Master establishes the technological capabilities of the campaign and determines which equipment is available.

## Cost, Availability, and Ownership

Three considerations determine whether a character can obtain and use a particular item.

**Cost:** The financial resources required to acquire the equipment.

**Availability:** Whether the equipment exists and can reasonably be obtained in the current era, location, and circumstances.

**Ownership:** Whether the character personally possesses the equipment or has permission to use it.

A character may have sufficient Wealth to purchase a military vehicle but lack the authority or opportunity to acquire one.

Likewise, a soldier may have access to expensive equipment issued by an organization without personally owning it.

These distinctions are particularly important in campaigns involving military service, criminal organizations, government agencies, wealthy patrons, or restricted technology.

The rules for Wealth, purchasing equipment, and issued equipment are described in Section 3.5.

## Equipment and Character Abilities

Equipment complements a character's Attributes, Skills, and Edges.

A skilled character can often accomplish more with appropriate equipment than an untrained character using the same tools.

However, some equipment requires specialized knowledge or training.

For example:

- Crafting may be used to manufacture or restore physical objects.
- Medicine may be used with medical equipment to treat injuries.
- Technology may be used to operate, diagnose, or repair complex technical systems.
- Vehicle may be used to control vehicles appropriate to the campaign.
- Close Combat and Ranged Combat are used with the appropriate weapons during combat.

The applicable Attribute, Skill, Difficulty, and equipment modifiers are determined by the action being attempted.

The Game Master should consider the equipment's capabilities, the character's familiarity with it, and the circumstances of its use.

Equipment should provide meaningful opportunities without replacing the importance of character abilities.

## Equipment and Resources

Equipment is not always permanently available.

Weapons require ammunition. Vehicles may require fuel and maintenance. Medical supplies can be consumed, and specialized tools may be damaged, lost, or stolen.

Characters may also encounter situations in which they must improvise with whatever resources are available.

The Game Master determines when resource tracking is important to the adventure.

A cinematic campaign may emphasize dramatic action and assume that ordinary supplies are available unless circumstances suggest otherwise.

A gritty survival campaign may require more careful attention to ammunition, provisions, fuel, equipment condition, and carrying capacity.

Both approaches use the same basic equipment rules, with the level of resource management adjusted to the campaign's intended style.

## Preparing for Adventure

Choosing equipment is an important part of preparing for an adventure.

Characters should consider the challenges they expect to face, the environment they will encounter, and the resources they can reasonably carry or obtain.

A heavily armed character may be poorly prepared for a wilderness expedition without food, shelter, or navigation equipment.

A skilled investigator may be unable to uncover important evidence without appropriate tools.

An experienced pilot may be helpless if a vehicle runs out of fuel or suffers a mechanical failure.

Equipment should encourage planning, resourcefulness, and creative problem-solving.

**The best-equipped adventurer is not necessarily the one carrying the most gear, but the one who brings the right tools for the challenges ahead.**
